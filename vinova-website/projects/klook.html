About Klook

Klook is a premier Asia-Pacific travel and experiences booking platform that connects travelers with local attractions, tour packages, cultural experiences, and transport services. Serving millions of global travelers, Klook enables seamless booking of live events, museum passes, and regional activity vouchers in real time.

How we helped

Vinova partnered with Klook to optimize mobile booking conversion paths and streamline ticket redemption UX across diverse international markets. We refined instant e-voucher generation flows, integrated fast multi-currency checkout options, and redesigned mobile search interfaces to help travelers effortlessly discover and book cultural attractions on the go.
