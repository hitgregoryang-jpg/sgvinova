About KooBits

KooBits is an award-winning personalized e-learning platform widely adopted across primary schools in Singapore and Southeast Asia. Built for young learners, it combines curriculum-aligned mathematics and science problem sets with gamified interactive challenges, helping students develop independent learning habits and problem-solving mastery.

How we helped

Vinova collaborated with KooBits to upgrade mobile platform responsiveness and optimize student engagement workflows. We streamlined interactive problem-solving interfaces, improved real-time synchronization between student practice modules and parent tracking portals, and enhanced UI components to deliver smooth, low-latency performance across mobile and tablet devices.
