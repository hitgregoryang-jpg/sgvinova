About Ninja Van

Ninja Van is a premier tech-enabled logistics company operating across Southeast Asia. The platform provides end-to-end express parcel delivery, last-mile supply chain management, and real-time shipment tracking for e-commerce sellers and individual consumers alike.

How we helped

Vinova worked alongside Ninja Van to modernize its mobile shipment tracking and pickup scheduling interfaces. We optimized driver dispatch workflows, integrated real-time push notifications for delivery status updates, and simplified parcel creation flows to enhance operational efficiency.
