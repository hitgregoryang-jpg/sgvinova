About ClassPass

ClassPass is a premier global subscription platform providing users access to thousands of fitness studios, gyms, spas, and wellness providers worldwide. The app allows fitness enthusiasts and active lifestyle seekers to seamlessly book workout classes, personal training, and wellness appointments using a flexible credit-based system.

How we helped

Vinova collaborated with ClassPass to optimize the mobile booking experience, enhancing real-time class schedule synchronization and credit management flows. We refined location-based studio recommendations, streamlined the reservation process, and upgraded the user interface to ensure fast, intuitive class discovery during peak booking windows.
