Frequently asked questions

Is my health data secure with Vinova?

We follow ISO 27001 information security practices and apply secure development standards, with access control and encryption designed in.

Can you build apps for medical devices?

Yes. We have built a human-machine interface (HMI) app for a portable medical device, deployed on the client's own hardware.

Do you design with Singapore's PDPA in mind?

Yes. We design for data protection and review the specific requirements with you for each project.
