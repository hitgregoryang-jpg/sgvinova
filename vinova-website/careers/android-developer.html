Android Developer

Singapore

Apply Now →

Role Overview

Build high-quality native Android apps for clients across industries. You will work in agile teams from design handoff to Google Play release.

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop and maintain native Android apps using Kotlin
  • Build responsive interfaces with Jetpack Compose or XML layouts
  • Integrate REST APIs, Firebase and third-party SDKs
  • Write clean, tested and maintainable code
  • Take part in code reviews and sprint planning
  • Publish apps and releases to Google Play

Requirements

  • Proven experience building Android apps with Kotlin
  • Solid knowledge of the Android SDK and Material Design
  • Experience with REST APIs, JSON and Git
  • Understanding of app architecture patterns such as MVVM
  • Good communication and teamwork

Bonus Points

  • Apps published on Google Play
  • Experience with Coroutines, Room or Hilt
  • Experience with unit and UI testing
  • Knowledge of mobile CI/CD
  • Exposure to Flutter or React Native

Why You Will Love Working Here

  • Work on real projects for clients across banking, healthcare, commerce and more
  • A friendly, close-knit team that values ethics and teamwork
  • Room to learn and grow alongside experienced colleagues
  • A modern, agile way of working
  • Compensation and benefits are discussed during the interview
