Website Designer

Singapore

Apply Now →

Role Overview

Design clear, modern websites that help our clients grow. You will take projects from wireframe to polished, responsive design and work closely with developers.

Key Responsibilities

  • Create wireframes, mockups and prototypes
  • Design responsive websites and landing pages
  • Apply and maintain brand guidelines across projects
  • Prepare design files and assets for developers
  • Support SEO-friendly page structure and layout
  • Gather feedback and refine designs

Requirements

  • A portfolio showing web or UI design work
  • Proficiency in Figma or similar tools
  • Understanding of responsive design, typography and layout
  • Basic understanding of UX principles
  • Good communication skills

Bonus Points

  • Basic HTML and CSS knowledge
  • Experience with WordPress or Webflow
  • Illustration, motion or graphic design skills
  • Understanding of SEO and accessibility

Why You Will Love Working Here

  • Work on real projects for clients across banking, healthcare, commerce and more
  • A friendly, close-knit team that values ethics and teamwork
  • Room to learn and grow alongside experienced colleagues
  • A modern, agile way of working
  • Compensation and benefits are discussed during the interview
