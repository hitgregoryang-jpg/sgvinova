Admin Coordinator

Singapore

Apply Now →

Role Overview

Support our app testing and client reporting work by monitoring user feedback, spotting trends and coordinating our development teams. You will be the link between what users say and what our teams build.

Key Responsibilities

  • Monitor app reviews and user feedback across all platforms
  • Categorise feedback: bugs, features, usability, sentiment, etc.
  • Identify trends and recurring issues
  • Prepare summary reports for our clients
  • Conduct hands-on user testing of partner apps (iOS & Android)
  • Coordinate weekly meetings with development teams

Requirements

  • Strong written English and clear reporting skills
  • Comfortable using both iOS and Android devices
  • Good attention to detail and an analytical mindset
  • Organised, with the ability to coordinate across teams
  • Able to work independently in a remote setup

Bonus Points

  • Experience in app testing, QA or customer support
  • Familiarity with App Store and Google Play review tools
  • Basic spreadsheet analysis or reporting experience

Salary & Benefits

  • Part-Time: $20/hour (12 hrs/week minimum)
  • Full-Time: $3,500–$4,500/month
  • Leave: 14 days annual + 14 days medical
  • Biannual performance reviews
  • Schedule: Monday – Friday, 9:30 AM – 5:30 PM
  • Work mode: WFH / Remote
