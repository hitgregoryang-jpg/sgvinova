About Snapask

Snapask is an EdTech platform offering 1-on-1 on-demand tutoring for primary and secondary school students across Asia. Students can snap a photo of a homework question and get matched instantly with qualified tutors for real-time explanations, learning materials, and interactive study courses.

How we helped

Vinova collaborated with the Snapask team to optimize the real-time tutor matching algorithm, speed up question submission workflows, and improve the chat and whiteboard UI/UX. We enhanced app responsiveness during peak study hours and streamlined step-by-step problem-solving features for better student engagement.
