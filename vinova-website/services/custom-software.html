Frequently asked questions

When should I choose custom software over a ready-made product?

When your process is unique, existing tools need heavy workarounds, or you want full control of the product.

Can you modernise our existing system?

Yes. We review the code and architecture, then upgrade or rebuild in stages to minimise disruption.

Who owns the source code?

Ownership is agreed in your contract. We are happy to discuss your requirements up front.
