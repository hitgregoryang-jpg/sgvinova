Frequently asked questions

What processes are suitable for RPA?

Rule-based, high-volume tasks such as data entry, reconciliation and report generation.

How long does an RPA project take?

A proof of concept can often be ready in a few weeks. We confirm timing after assessment.

Is RPA secure?

Yes. Bots run with controlled access and audit logs, following your security policies.
