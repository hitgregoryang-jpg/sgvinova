Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between UI and UX design?

UX is how a product works and feels to use; UI is how it looks. We design both together.

Do you redesign existing apps?

Yes. We audit your current product, find friction points and deliver an improved design.

Can you design before we choose a developer?

Yes, though designing and building with one team helps ensure the final product matches the design.
